Privacy Policy – Video Robot

Last updated: 2026-11-13

What the app does

Video Robot is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. It uploads videos that the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

The app requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information (youtube.readonly) so it can confirm which channel it is connected to. It does not read, collect or analyse any other Google user data.

Storage

The OAuth access token is stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server operated by the app, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google policies

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner at the support email listed on the Google consent screen.
